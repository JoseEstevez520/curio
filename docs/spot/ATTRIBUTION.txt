MUSIC — NONE in the current render (SFX-only).
  "Digital Lemonade" (Kevin MacLeod) was removed at the owner's request.
  The final music track will be chosen and muxed later.

  Pipeline is ready to drop in a track:
    1. Put the chosen file at  curio-spot/music.mp3
    2. The build step decodes it to awork/music.wav
    3. mix.py auto-detects it and adds it as a LOW bed (peak ~0.13) with
       SIDECHAIN DUCKING — the music dips (to ~55-60%, i.e. ~ -5 to -6 dB) under
       each meaningful SFX and fades back over ~1 s. No code changes needed.
  Whatever track is chosen, record its title / artist / source / license here.

MIX / LEVELS
