Heliocentric

A model of the solar system with the Sun at its center, around which all planets orbit. It is the opposite of the geocentric model, which places a stationary Earth at the center.

16th c.
Century when Copernicus reframed the model as a scientific theory, displacing geocentrism.
Heliocentric vs. Geocentric
Heliocentric
  • Sun at center
  • Earth is a planet in orbit
  • Retrograde loops explained naturally
Geocentric
  • Earth at center
  • Everything orbits Earth
  • Retrograde loops required elaborate epicycles
